You are inside the board. Sand above, sand below, sand all around — three layers of blocks and a shotgun. Every block touches 26 others (3×3×3 minus itself), so the number you uncover counts mines in your own plane, in the ceiling and in the floor. An arrow pointing up means a mine sits in one of the 9 blocks overhead. Pointing down, in the floor. Get it wrong once and the whole cave turns to glass.

3D Minesweeper is that and nothing else: a board, no enemies, and every one of them solvable by thinking. Dungeon Mode is the same rules somewhere that fights back — a descent through mine pockets, with things living in the rock, won by flagging every mine in the place.
