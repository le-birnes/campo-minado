MinesweeperThree Planes

You are inside the board. Sand above, sand below, sand all around — three layers of blocks and a staff. Every block touches 26 others (3×3×3 minus itself), so the number you uncover counts mines in your own plane, in the ceiling and in the floor. An arrow pointing up means a mine sits in one of the 9 blocks overhead. Pointing down, in the floor. Get it wrong once and the whole cave turns to glass.
